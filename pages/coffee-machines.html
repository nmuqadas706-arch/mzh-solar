How to Choose the Right Coffee Machine for Your Home or Workplace

1. Manual Pump Espresso

Ideal for home baristas who enjoy grinding fresh coffee, tamping portafilters, and manually steaming velvety microfoam with a swivel steam wand (e.g. De'Longhi Dedica, Breville Barista Express).

2. Fully Automatic Bean-to-Cup

Grinds, tamps, extracts, and dispenses espresso or Americano with a single button press. Perfect for busy mornings and office conference rooms (e.g. Philips 2200, De'Longhi Magnifica S).

3. Capsule / Pod Systems

Zero mess, zero cleanup, and maximum consistency. Takes up minimal counter space while delivering rich 19-bar extraction in under 30 seconds (e.g. Nespresso Essenza Mini).
