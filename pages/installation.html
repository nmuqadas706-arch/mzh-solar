DISCO Net-Metering

Export Surplus Electricity with Bi-Directional Green Metering

Net metering allows residential and commercial solar owners in Pakistan to feed excess solar electricity generated during the day back into the utility grid (LESCO, IESCO, KE, FESCO, GEPCO, MEPCO). At the end of the billing month, units exported are offset against units imported, radically reducing or eliminating your monthly electricity bill.
