Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions About Packages

Key answers to help you choose the best solar capacity for your home or business in Pakistan.

Can a 6kW solar system run a 1.5-ton Inverter AC?
Yes, absolutely. A 6kW system produces between 25 to 30 units of electricity per day during peak sunshine hours. It can easily power a 1.5-ton inverter air conditioner during daytime while simultaneously running a refrigerator, deep freezer, LED lights, fans, and surveillance cameras with zero load-shedding.
What is the difference between Hybrid and On-Grid solar packages?
Hybrid Systems connect to both solar panels, grid electricity, and battery storage. If WAPDA fails, the hybrid inverter switches seamlessly to battery backup without turning off your appliances. On-Grid Systems do not have batteries; they export excess solar units directly to WAPDA via Net Metering to deduct your electricity bill, but shut down during grid outages for lineman safety.
How long does the DISCO Net-Metering process take in Pakistan?
The standard DISCO (LESCO, IESCO, FESCO, MEPCO, KE) Green Meter process typically takes 4 to 8 weeks from the date of initial application submission. MZH Solar handles all technical documentation, AEDB certification filing, DISCO inspection, and meter commissioning on your behalf.
Why choose Lithium (LiFePO4) over Lead-Acid / Tubular batteries?
LiFePO4 (Lithium Iron Phosphate) batteries offer 6,000+ deep cycles (10-15 years lifetime), zero maintenance (no acid or distilled water topping), 95% depth of discharge, compact wall-mount footprint, and 2-3 hours rapid recharge. Tubular batteries last 2-3 years, require frequent acid maintenance, and take 8-10 hours to recharge.
What warranties are provided with MZH Solar packages?
All our turnkey systems include a 25-year linear power warranty on solar modules (minimum 84.8% performance at year 25), 5-year manufacturer warranty on hybrid/on-grid inverters, 5 to 10-year warranty on Lithium batteries (or 1-year on tubular), and 1-year comprehensive on-site installation and workmanship guarantee.
