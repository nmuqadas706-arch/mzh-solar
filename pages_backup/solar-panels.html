Monofacial vs Bifacial Solar Panels: What is Better for Your Roof?

In Pakistan, rooftops with white tile or reflective coatings benefit immensely from Bifacial Dual-Glass panels, which capture sunlight from both sides and deliver up to 15%–25% extra generation. For slanted tile roofs or sheds with low rear clearance, Monofacial HPBC modules deliver the highest frontal wattage per square foot.
